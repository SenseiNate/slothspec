SMART PET FEEDER (SPF) SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION
Rev A, DRAFT

3.1 Feeding

SPF-001 The SPF shall dispense food at scheduled times.
SPF-002 The SPF shall dispense a portion of 20 to 200 grams, adjustable in 5 gram increments.
SPF-003 The SPF shall dispense food quickly and quietly.
SPF-004 The SPF should notify the owner when the hopper is low.
SPF-005 The SPF shall store TBD days of food.
SPF-006 The SPF shall detect jams and shall attempt to clear them and shall notify the owner if the jam persists.
SPF-007 The hopper shall be easy to clean.
SPF-008 It shall support multiple pets as appropriate.

3.2 Connectivity

SPF-010 The SPF shall connect to the owner's home Wi-Fi network using WPA2 or WPA3.
SPF-011 The mobile app will display feeding history, hopper level, device status, etc.
SPF-012 The SPF shall send notifications in near real-time.
SPF-013 The SPF shall continue dispensing on schedule during a loss of network connectivity of up to 72 hours.
SPF-014 Firmware updates shall be applied.
SPF-015 The SPF shall minimize power consumption.

3.3 Safety and Environment

SPF-020 The SPF shall not dispense more than 3 portions within any 60 minute period.
SPF-021 The SPF shall operate in ambient temperatures from 0 °C to 40 °C.
SPF-022 The SPF shall be robust to tampering by pets, including but not limited to pawing, tipping, and chewing.
SPF-023 The SPF shall operate on battery backup for 48 hours if possible.
SPF-024 All exterior surfaces in contact with food shall be made of food-safe materials and/or BPA-free plastic.
SPF-025 The SPF shall withstand a drop of 1 onto a hard floor.
